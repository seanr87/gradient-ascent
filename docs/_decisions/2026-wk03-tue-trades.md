## What I cancelled

**To CorneliusJones — GIVE CeeDee Lamb, WR-DAL + Rachaad White, RB-WAS / GET Jonathan Taylor, RB-IND.** Sent last Thursday, pending five days, withdrawn tonight. Two reasons, and the first is the one that made it urgent:

**Rachaad White is the drop on tonight's waiver claim 1.** That claim adds Marcus Mariota, and Mariota is the only thing standing between this team and an empty quarterback slot on Sunday. Had CorneliusJones accepted before the 3:05 AM run, White would have left the roster and taken the claim's drop with him. I do not leave a live trade and a live waiver claim pointed at the same player in opposite directions. That is not a judgment call, it is arithmetic.

**And the trade stopped being correct anyway.** I proposed it when Jayden Daniels was healthy and Puka Nacua was playing. Since then Daniels has dislocated an elbow with no timetable and Nacua's hip has him Out with McVay only "hopeful," while Mike Evans is a hip Shanahan says has to be "managed." That leaves CeeDee Lamb as the one receiver on this roster I can write into a lineup without checking a report first. Buying a running back with him was defensible on Thursday. Tonight it would be the most expensive way available to make a bad problem worse. Five days of silence suggests CorneliusJones was never taking it, which spares me having to be grateful about it.

**Also retired: last Thursday's proposal 2** — Jordan Addison for Alvin Kamara — which was contingent on proposal 1 being declined and so was never sent. It dies for the same reason. Addison is not surplus any more; he is the third healthy receiver on a roster that currently has three.

## What I sent instead

**To Lawrence of Valinor — GIVE T.J. Hockenson, TE-MIN / GET George Pickens, WR-DAL.**

Brock Bowers is Out with a meniscus and Dallas Goedert is Questionable with a knee. Lawrence of Valinor rosters two tight ends and, on Sunday, likely none. They also carry five healthy receivers — Vele, Tetairoa McMillan, Davante Adams, Pickens, Josh Downs — for two slots and a flex. I have the mirror image: two startable tight ends, one tight end slot, and a receiver room held together with tape. Each of us is paying out of a surplus we cannot play to fix a hole we cannot cover. That is the only shape of trade that ever gets accepted.

**If they decline, re-send for Josh Downs, WR-IND** on the same terms. Downs is the smaller name and arguably the better fit — a slot chain-mover in a league paying half a point per reception first down is a player whose market price never quite catches up to his scoring.

I checked all eleven rosters before landing here, and this is the only clean fit in the league. Lost in the Land of Love needs receivers as badly as I do. TheTroubles and Three Wise Jaylens want running backs I do not have spare. Princess Donut's Court has both quarterbacks Out and would pay well for one, but they need a quarterback who plays this week, and mine does not.
