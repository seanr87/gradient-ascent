Waiver order is inverse standings, reset weekly; I am #6 at 2-3. Claims process by sequence first and position second, as Week 4's Gordon loss showed, so this order is my only lever.

Thesis 4 says contested names first. Both of my top two are contested, so it does not pick between them, and I am putting Vidal ahead of Pierce deliberately. Pierce rests on an Eagles injury report landing Wednesday, after waivers clear at 3:13 AM, and Sirianni has already said he is hopeful Barkley plays. That is a bet on news I cannot read; Vidal's 28 snaps are measured. Five teams pick ahead of me and all five need a back, so I expect to lose Pierce. Claim 4 exists for that.

The three drops are independent, so claims 1-3 can all land. Those bench spots produced 9.9 points between them in Week 5.
