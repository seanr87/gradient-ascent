## Contingencies

### Puka Nacua, Questionable, Monday 8:15 PM

This is the file. Everything else is typing.

Nacua carries a hip designation in the 13:10 UTC pull. Sean McVay called it groin soreness that showed up after Thursday's practice; Nacua did not practice Friday or Saturday, and the Rams are calling him a pre-game decision. He has one extra day the rest of the league does not.

**He starts.** The alternative is Jordan Addison, who scored 0.00 in Week 1 for a Minnesota offense whose quarterback situation put Carson Wentz on the trending-adds list, and this scoring pays half a point per reception plus another half every time it moves the chains — which is a description of Puka Nacua's job and not of Addison's. A Questionable target hog at sixty percent to play beats a healthy fourth receiver. That is the manual's test and it is not close.

The problem is not who is better. The problem is **the clock**. Inactives post about 6:45 PM ET Monday. By then Addison, Hockenson, Lamb and Evans have all played. If Nacua is ruled out Monday evening, the WR slot is a zero and there is no living player to put in it. A contingency that cannot be executed is not a contingency.

So I am buying one, and it costs a bench body I have twice called droppable:

```
ADD Konata Mumpfield, WR-LAR / DROP Roschon Johnson, RB-CHI
```

**Mumpfield plays in Nacua's game, on Nacua's team, on Monday night.** He is the hedge that is worth the most in exactly the world where I need it: Rams receiver Jordan Whittington is **Doubtful**, so if Nacua also sits, the Rams' Week 2 receiver room is Davante Adams, Mumpfield, Tutu Atwell and a sixth-rounder. Mumpfield ran 26% of the snaps behind a healthy room; he does not stay at 26% with the two men above him out. If Nacua plays, Mumpfield sits on my bench and costs me a roster spot I was using to store Roschon Johnson, who locks at 1:00 today and was never going to be started again.

**This add must be executed before 1:00 PM ET.** Not because Mumpfield locks then — his game is Monday — but because the wire is first-come and eleven other managers can read a doubtful tag. Do it this morning.

**If the add does not happen, Nacua still starts.** I am not benching him for Addison as a consolation. A sixty percent chance of eighteen points is worth more than a certainty of seven, and if the hedge fails to get filed I would rather take the exposure than pay for insurance I did not buy.
