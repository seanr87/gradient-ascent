My quarterback dislocated his elbow and there is no timetable. I do not roster a second one. Everything below follows from that sentence.

## Claims (enter in this order)

```
ADD Marcus Mariota, QB-WAS / DROP Rachaad White, RB-WAS
ADD Emanuel Wilson, RB-SEA / DROP Konata Mumpfield, WR-LAR
ADD Adonai Mitchell, WR-NYJ / DROP Konata Mumpfield, WR-LAR
```

1. **Mariota** — the only quarterback on this roster is Out with no return date. This is not an upgrade, it is a vacancy.
2. **Emanuel Wilson** — 21 carries in Week 2 and the back ahead of him left with a chest injury. Volume is king and this is the week's largest pile of it.
3. **Adonai Mitchell** — 12 targets, 29.3% of his quarterback's attempts. **Conditional backup to claim 2**: it reuses the same drop, so Sleeper cancels it if Wilson lands. That is deliberate.

## Reasoning

**The order is the whole decision.** I sit at waiver #8, which normally means I am shopping after seven other managers. This week it means something better, and it is worth showing the work, because it is the reason Mariota goes first instead of the best player available:

| Waiver | Team | Quarterbacks rostered |
|---|---|---|
| #1 | Lost in the Land of Love | Mahomes, Kyler Murray [Out] |
| #2 | CorneliusJones | Maye, Purdy |
| #3 | What can Brown do for u? | Nix, Herbert |
| #4 | Three Wise Jaylens | Mayfield, Hurts |
| #5 | Sea Squirts | Stafford, Burrow |
| #6 | Lawrence of Valinor | Trevor Lawrence |
| #7 | The Wizard's Apprentice | Dak Prescott |
| **#8** | **Gradient Ascent** | **Jayden Daniels [Out]** |
| #9 | Princess Donut's Court | Caleb Williams [Out], Jaxson Dart [Out] |

Not one team ahead of me has a quarterback problem. The one team in this league with a worse quarterback situation than mine — Princess Donut's Court, both of them Out — picks at #9, immediately behind me. I am first in line for the only position I actually have to fill, by one slot, for one week.

That single slot is why Mariota is claim 1 and not claim 2. This league uses rolling priority: win a claim and I fall to #12. If I put Emanuel Wilson first and somehow won him, I would drop behind Princess Donut's Court and hand them the quarterback I cannot go without. Claiming the better player first is how I end up starting nobody at quarterback. The cheap claim goes first precisely because it is the one I cannot afford to lose.

**Mariota** is also the right quarterback and not merely the available one. Dan Quinn named him the Week 3 starter while Daniels gets second opinions on the elbow. He runs — he cleared 40 rushing yards in four starts last season — and this scoring pays half a point per rushing first down while docking two per interception and paying only four per passing touchdown. That formula is unkind to pocket passers and generous to exactly Mariota's profile. He draws Seattle, which is a genuinely bad matchup, and he is a fringe top-20 quarterback rather than a solution. I am not claiming him because he is good. I am claiming him because the alternative is an empty slot, and because his value and my need expire on the same day: the week Daniels is cleared is the week Mariota stops starting. The hedge unwinds itself.

**Emanuel Wilson** is the best player I can put a claim on. He out-touched Jadarian Price 21 to 13 in Week 2, Price left that game with a chest injury, and Wilson could take the clear majority of a backfield in Week 3. Twenty-one carries with a half-point bonus on every rushing first down is the most valuable shape a player can have in this format. He is also the consensus add of the week — 2.7 million across Sleeper, the single most-added player in fantasy football — and I will be at #12 by the time this claim is evaluated. I am filing it anyway because a failed claim costs nothing and the ones that go unclaimed clear to free agency, where Wednesday can pick them up without spending priority. That is exactly how Hockenson got here last week.

**Adonai Mitchell** covers the receiver room rather than the backfield. Nacua is hip-Out and McVay is only "hopeful"; Evans has a hip Shanahan says must "be managed." Neither has been ruled out and I am not panicking about either, but Lamb and Addison are the only two receivers I can currently write into a lineup with confidence. Mitchell led the Jets in targets with 12 on 61 of 74 snaps, and Omar Cooper's ankle may concentrate that further. I rank him behind Wilson because the backfield is thinner than it looks once White is gone — Irving, Skattebo and Justice Hill is three backs for two slots and a flex — and because he shares a drop with claim 2 on purpose. If both landed I would owe a second body, and the only ones left are Justice Hill and Hockenson. I am not gutting the bench chasing two contested players I probably win neither of.

**The drops.** Rachaad White is the worst player I roster, and the injury creating this whole problem is the same one that hollows out his offense: Washington without Daniels is a worse place to be a backup running back. He goes first so that if only one claim lands, it costs me the least. Konata Mumpfield was bought for one specific purpose — insurance on Nacua for a Monday night game that has now been played. That purpose expired on schedule. He is not being punished for a bad week; his job simply ended. If all three claims fail he stays, which is fine.

**Passed on, and why:**

- **C.J. Stroud** (174k adds, oddly cheap) — a real starting quarterback available for less noise than Mariota. He is a pocket passer, and this scoring taxes that archetype twice over. I would rather have the worse quarterback who runs than the better one who does not, which is a sentence only this rulebook could produce.
- **Kirk Cousins, Jameis Winston** — immobile, and Winston in a minus-two-per-interception league is a liability I would be paying to roster.
- **Bryce Young** (1.2m adds) — plausible, and genuinely close to Mariota. Mariota wins on a confirmed start in a scheme that just produced elite quarterback scoring, and on the timeline matching Daniels' absence exactly.
- **Rashod Bateman** (127k adds) — the tempting cheap one, and the reason I checked: he played 80% of snaps and drew *one* target on 26 routes. Volume is king and he has routes, not volume. Baltimore ran it 37 times to 25 throws. Snap share is not opportunity.
- **Zach Ertz, Darren Waller, Terrance Ferguson, Oronde Gadsden** — I already roster two tight ends, which is one more than the format needs.
- **Chase McLaughlin** (364k adds) — distance scoring makes kickers real, which is why I drafted a big leg in Baltimore. Tyler Loop is the version of this I already own.
- **Detroit, New York Giants, New Orleans, Las Vegas, Carolina defenses** — streaming by matchup is correct in this league and I will keep doing it, but a defense swap is a free-agent move I can make Friday for nothing. Spending a waiver claim on one while my quarterback slot is empty would be embarrassing.

**One piece of history that is not decoration.** This team has filed claims for two straight weeks and had zero of them reach Sleeper; the Week 2 review documented all five going missing. What did land, both times, were the Wednesday free-agent adds — Hockenson and Mumpfield are on this roster right now because of them. So the pipeline works when the lines are simple and the deadline is soft. This one is neither: waivers process at **3:14 AM ET**, the lines are above, and if they are not entered tonight, claim 1 does not fail — it never happens, and I start Week 3 with no quarterback at all.
