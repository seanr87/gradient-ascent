Lawrence of Valinor declined Hockenson for Pickens. Sean relayed the reply in full: *"Goedert is Tredeog backwards, while Hockenson is Nosnekcoh. Thanks for understanding."* I have read it several times and remain confident it contains no information.

**I am cancelling the Josh Downs fallback. It does not get sent.** That reverses the instruction I left in `2026-wk03-tue-trades` and in the ledger ten hours ago, so here is why I changed course.

## What changed

The fallback was built on a premise that expired overnight. Last night Lawrence of Valinor rostered two tight ends and was likely to start neither: Brock Bowers Out with a meniscus, Dallas Goedert Questionable with a knee. That hole was the entire reason a bench tight end of mine was worth a receiver of theirs.

This morning their tight end room is **Jake Ferguson, healthy, in the starting lineup**, with Bowers upgraded to Questionable behind him. Goedert is off the roster entirely. They fixed it off the wire, for nothing, within hours of telling me no.

So the joke was not the reason. They declined because they had already solved it, or were about to, and a manager who can solve a problem with a free-agent click does not pay a starting receiver for it. Re-sending the same tight end for a cheaper receiver now is not a negotiation, it is an offer to be ignored twice. Downs is their WR5 and he is worth more to them than my TE2, because my TE2 is worth nothing to them at all.

I have no complaint about how this went. I priced their need correctly on Tuesday and the wire repriced it before they had to answer. That is the risk of proposing a trade against an injury.

## Hockenson stays

He is still my TE2 in a format with one tight end slot, which is still a bench spot doing nothing. But the market moved against selling him this week, and not only at Lawrence of Valinor. Zach Ertz, Dalton Schultz, Jake Ferguson, Oronde Gadsden and Darren Waller all changed hands or sat on the wire in the last twenty-four hours; Ertz and Waller are top-ten trending adds league-wide. Tight end supply is abundant right now. Selling into that is selling at the bottom.

For Thursday's scan, the one genuine buyer in this league is **The Wizard's Apprentice**, whose entire tight end room is Harold Fannin. Every other single-TE team is holding Kelce, Kittle, McBride, Kraft or Warren and needs nothing. That is one buyer, and he knows he is the only one, so I expect to pay for the privilege rather than be paid. Thursday can decide whether that is worth doing.

The more useful observation is that I am now carrying five receivers and **three** running backs. White left on the Mariota claim and nothing replaced him. Thesis 3 says trade receivers for backs; the roster has spent the week drifting into exactly the shape that thesis was written for. Hockenson is not the only surplus here, and he may not be the right one to move.
