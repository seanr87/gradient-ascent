## Reasoning

**Dalton Kincaid, TE-BUF — START.**

He has no injury designation in tonight's pull and none on the Bills' final Week 2 report. In Week 1 he caught five of six targets for 130 yards and scored 17.50, which was the single cleanest piece of my 140.46. The game is set at a 54.5-point total, the highest on the Week 2 board, with Buffalo favored by 5.5 — a script where both offenses throw and Kincaid's target share sits in the middle of it. Under this scoring, a tight end running chain-moving routes in a shootout is paid three times: 0.1 a yard, half a point for the catch, another half every time it converts.

Starting him locks the TE slot before I see the Sunday slate, so name the alternative: **T.J. Hockenson, TE-MIN**, added off free agency yesterday for Darren Waller. I said on Wednesday that Hockenson is a bench upgrade over Waller and not a challenge to Kincaid, and one day has not changed that. Hockenson plays Sunday in a Minnesota offense whose quarterback situation is bad enough that 166,524 Sleeper managers added Carson Wentz in 24 hours. Kincaid plays tonight in the highest-total game of the week with a healthy Josh Allen. The comparison is not close, and the thing I am giving up by locking early — the option to see Sunday's news first — is worth less than the difference between those two situations.

**Buffalo, DEF-BUF — START.**

This is the honest one. A 54.5 total is a bad number for a defense, and Buffalo gave up 31 to Houston in Week 1. I looked hard at the alternative and I am naming it rather than pretending it doesn't exist: bench Buffalo tonight, then drop him Sunday morning for one of the nineteen unrostered defenses — San Francisco, Detroit, Green Bay and Chicago are all free — and stream a better matchup.

I am not doing it, for three reasons. First, benching Buffalo tonight without making that add is a guaranteed zero in the DEF slot, and this file only issues start/bench lines, not roster moves; a half-executed plan is worse than either whole one. Second, the drop is permanent and the gain is one week — I would be cutting a top unit at 5 PM on a Thursday to chase a matchup I haven't scored. Third, the matchup is less bad than the total suggests: Detroit has its starting right tackle (Blake Miller, knee) and starting left guard (Christian Mahogany, hip) ruled out, which is two fifths of a pass-protection unit missing against Buffalo's rush, and a team trailing a 5.5-point favorite throws. Sacks and takeaways are where a defense's points actually come from in this format, and both are live tonight.

If Buffalo gets shredded anyway, that is a Week 3 streaming decision made with evidence instead of a guess, and I will make it in Sunday's file or next Tuesday's. I am not making it now on the strength of a betting total.

**On my opponent.** The Wizard's Apprentice starts **James Cook, RB-BUF** tonight, and Buffalo listing running back Ty Johnson questionable (hamstring) points Cook's workload up, not down. So I will be watching a game where my opponent's back and my tight end share a sideline, which is a tidy way of saying the variance tonight runs both directions. He is 0-1 with 102.10 points for; I would rather bank Kincaid's floor than hope Cook has a quiet one.
