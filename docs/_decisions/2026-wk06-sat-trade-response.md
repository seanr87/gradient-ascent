## Why

- **Decline, on arithmetic before talent.** I am at 14 of 14 with no IR slot, so accepting means cutting four bodies — in practice Tuesday's entire waiver haul plus one more. The real trade is Nacua and four of mine for the best two of theirs. That is not depth, it is a storage problem.
- **Nacua is not surplus.** R1 pick, 33.7 in Week 5, my top scorer, Thesis 1 in person. Lesson 5 says sell surplus, not starters, and Tuesday's claims already took my receiver room from six to three.
- **Counter: Kincaid for Keenan Allen.** One-for-one, roster-neutral, nobody drops anybody. Kincaid has handed me 3.2 and 3.3 while Higbee out-targets him 17 to zero, so the second tight end is the genuine spare. Allen has seen nine targets in each of the last two weeks with Alec Pierce on IR, and draws Tennessee.
- **Not Stroud, not today.** The ledger's QB thread says one of Daniels and Stroud is surplus once a QB-short manager engages, and WCHolland rosters exactly one quarterback. I am still not selling the hedge the day before a Sunday.

Passed on:
- **Ladd McConkey** — the one name I would genuinely pay for, currently a foot injury with 183 yards across three or four games. Not a Nacua return alone.
- **Ollie Gordon** — Questionable, and I claimed Braelon Allen in Week 4 precisely as his backup.
- **Will Shipley** — IR, torn plantar fascia, no IR slot here. A dead spot I would be paying to rent.
